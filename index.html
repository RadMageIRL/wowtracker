        WoW Tracker v0.1.1

        A World of Warcraft (Retail) dashboard for resets and scheduled events: when the next daily and weekly resets are, what is in its scheduled window now, and what starts next, in your local time.

        Scheduled, not live

        Every time here comes from a published schedule or a fixed game rule. The game offers no public live-event feed, so nothing on this page is read from the game. During maintenance or a hotfix a realm can be early or late.

        Labels

        • Confirmed times have an official Blizzard source or a documented game rule. They carry no label.
        • Predicted times follow a pattern from earlier months or years that Blizzard has not published for these dates yet. They are always labeled.
        • Activities with no verified schedule are not shown as times at all; they are listed under the calendar.

        Privacy

        Settings, stars, hidden events, reminders and your checklist are saved in this browser only (Back up settings, in the footer, copies them). Two things leave it: the clock check asks this site's own address for the time, and Latest news asks this site for Blizzard's news list (not while you have it collapsed). Blizzard sees this site's request, not yours.

          What to export

            A calendar file (.ics) is made in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Open it with your calendar app, or import it (Google Calendar: on a computer, Settings, Import). Times are in UTC, so your calendar shows them in your own time zone. Each event keeps the same identifier in every export, so a calendar that matches identifiers updates it instead of adding a copy.

            Suggested tasks

            Common Retail activities, to start a list quickly. Pick only what you do: not every one fits every character, and none of them is checked against the game. Rename or delete them any time with Edit.

            Back up settings

            Your settings, stars, hidden events, reminders and checklist are kept in this browser only. Clearing site data removes them, and Safari can remove them by itself after 7 days of using Safari without a visit here. A backup is one line of text you keep; nothing is sent anywhere.
